# Getting help

## Support

- **Support form:** [balane.app/en/support](https://www.balane.app/en/support) — pick your app, describe the problem, we answer by email.
- **Email:** [contact@balane.tech](mailto:contact@balane.tech)

## Reporting a bug

Two ways, both end up in the same place:

1. Use the [support form](https://www.balane.app/en/support), or
2. [Open an issue](https://github.com/JHBalane/Balane-Releases/issues/new) directly in this repository.

Every confirmed bug is tracked publicly: it appears on the
[status page](https://www.balane.app/en/status) the moment we file it, moves to
*In progress* while we work on it, and stays listed as *Fixed* once the fix has
shipped — so you can always see what is known and what happened to it.

### What to include

- **Which app and version** — shown in the app's About window.
- **Your OS and version** — e.g. macOS 15.2 or Windows 11.
- **What you did, what you expected, what happened instead.**
- A screenshot, if the problem is visible.

## Feature requests

Also welcome — open an issue and describe what you are trying to do (not just
the feature you have in mind; knowing the goal lets us solve it properly).
